# Disclaimer

ai-health-tracking is a personal project shared free of charge, as is, with no warranty.

- **Not advice.** Nothing it produces is medical, dietetic, nutritional or training advice. Targets are general starting estimates, not a prescription.
- **Estimates.** Food values are estimated by an AI from descriptions and photos, and wearable calorie figures carry an uncertainty of roughly 10-15%. Use trends, not single numbers.
- **See a professional first** if you have a medical condition, take medication that affects appetite, weight or blood sugar, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, or have a history of disordered eating.
- **Your data, your responsibility.** Your food and health data live in your own Google Sheet and Claude account. Keep the sheet private, and keep your Oura client secret to yourself.
- **Third-party services.** Oura, Google, Strava and Anthropic can change their APIs, terms or prices at any time, which may stop parts of this from working.
